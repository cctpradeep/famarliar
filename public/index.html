Trained counsellors, not just a couple with opinions.

We know how heavy this feels. When a marriage is in trouble, everyone has advice, and most of it comes from people who love you but have never sat with a couple in crisis. We have.

Between us we hold a master's degree and a diploma in marriage and family counselling, and Pradeep is currently studying for an MDiv. We have been married 13 years, and we have two children. So we bring both the training and the lived experience of a marriage that has had to be worked at.

That combination is the whole reason Famarliar exists. Structured counselling, grounded in real clinical practice, delivered by two people who will treat you like a couple rather than a case.

What the first call is not.

Most people hesitate because they imagine the worst version of this conversation. So here is what will not happen.

  • You will not be asked to explain everything. Almost nobody shares the real issue in the first 30 minutes, and we don't expect you to.
  • Nobody takes sides. We are not there to decide who was right about the fight.
  • Nothing is recorded, and nothing leaves the room. Confidentiality is the first thing we will talk about.
  • Nobody will tell you what to decide about your marriage. That decision stays yours.
  • It is not a sales call. If we are not the right help for you, we will say so and point you somewhere better.

How working with us actually goes.

Four stages, and you decide at every one whether to take the next.

1

A free 30-minute call

We meet, you get a feel for us, and we answer whatever you need answered. Share only what you're comfortable sharing. Costs nothing.

2

An assessment

Before any counselling begins, we take proper time to understand your situation. This means your sessions are spent working on the marriage rather than explaining it from scratch. This stage is paid, one time.

3

Your first block of sessions

Once the assessment is done and you're ready, we work together across a minimum of five sessions. A block, not a single conversation, because rebuilding takes more than one sitting.

4

Continuing as you need

After the first five, couples carry on session by session for as long as it helps. Many need fewer than they feared.

Questions people ask before they book.

Is this really confidential?

Yes. What is said in our sessions stays there. We don't discuss clients, we don't record sessions, and nobody in your family, church or workplace hears anything from us. For many couples this is the single biggest worry, and it is the first thing we cover on the free call.

What if my spouse won't come?

Come on your own. Reaching out by yourself is a real place to start, and it is often the most motivated partner who makes the first move. We will work with you as you are, and if your partner becomes willing later, we can bring them in then.

Does needing counselling mean our marriage has failed?

No. Couples who ask for help are usually the ones who care most about staying together. Getting help early is what strong marriages do, and the couples who wait are the ones who run out of options.

What does it cost?

The first 30-minute call is free. If you decide to go further, we explain every cost plainly on that call, with no obligation and no surprises later. Nothing is charged until you have chosen to continue.

What if we've left it too late?

We won't promise you that every marriage can be rebuilt, because that wouldn't be honest. What we can say is that couples who still care enough to be afraid usually have more to work with than they think. A free call will tell you more than guessing will.

Do you meet online or in person?

Both. Sessions run online, which many couples prefer for privacy and convenience, or in person in Byrathi, Bangalore. The exact location is shared once your call is booked.

What languages do you counsel in?

English mainly, and we also counsel in Tamil and Kannada. You shouldn't have to translate your own marriage.

How soon can we start?

Message us on WhatsApp and we'll find a time for the free call, usually within 24 to 48 hours.
