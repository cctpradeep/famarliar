About Famarliar.

Two trained counsellors, married 13 years, who would rather help you rebuild than watch another marriage quietly end.

Who we are

We are Pradeep and Gomathi Mcwin. Between us we hold a master's degree and a diploma in marriage and family counselling, and Pradeep is currently studying for an MDiv at the Centre for Global Learning and Development, Bangalore. We work with couples, families and individuals across Bangalore and online.

We have been married 13 years and have a son and a daughter. We say that not as a credential but as context. We know what it costs to keep choosing each other through hard seasons, and we don't counsel from a distance.

What we believe about this work

That most marriages in trouble are not short of love. They are short of tools, and short of a safe place to use them.

That confidentiality is not a policy line, it is the foundation. Nothing you tell us travels.

That people deserve honesty. If we are not the right help for you, we will say so rather than sell you sessions.
